How a chapter works

  1. Same brand, your campus.

    Every chapter runs under the We Just Chattin name, logo and format, and publishes to the main channels, so a clip from your Yard reaches our whole audience.

  2. A small team.

    A chapter lead, a host and someone on camera and edits. That’s enough to film weekly and run one event a month.

  3. Events, not just videos.

    Walk clubs, live rooms and paired conversations. Free, open to everyone, listed on Posh.

  4. We back you.

    Playbook, question banks, editing templates, sponsor intros and a chapter page right here.
