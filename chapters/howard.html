About the chapter

We Just Chattin started with a mic, a spatula and a camera on Howard’s campus in Washington, DC. The Howard chapter keeps filming: street interviews on the Yard, debates between strangers, and the honest, funny, sometimes uncomfortable answers that made the brand.

Off camera, the chapter runs free events for Howard students and the wider DMV: walk clubs on the National Mall, live rooms, and paired conversations where you skip the small talk. Everything publishes under the main We Just Chattin channels.

Events & activities

Howard chapter events are free and built for students and young professionals across the DMV. Come solo or bring a friend.

  • Walk N’ Talk. A paired walk on the National Mall with a stranger and a question.
  • Street interviews. Filmed on the Yard. If you see the spatula, come say something.
  • Live rooms. Group conversations on the topics our clips start.
  • Sponsor activations. Campus takeovers with partners like Buttah Skin and Capital City Mambo Sauce.
